Questions about RevOps consulting

What does a RevOps consultant do?

A RevOps (Revenue Operations) consultant makes the CRM, the sales process and the reporting work as one system, so sales, marketing and leadership read the same numbers. In practice I audit the CRM, fix the pipeline and required fields, automate repetitive work and build the dashboards leadership uses every week.

What is included in a CRM audit?

A written diagnosis (data quality, stages, required fields, automations, reports), a prioritized action plan with the time each fix takes, a live readout and a recorded walkthrough. I also interview 2 to 4 people who use the CRM daily, and an express version is available.

How much does a RevOps consultant cost?

I don’t publish prices. After a first call I send a written list of needs, then a fixed quote with the scope, the deliverables, what is not included and one price that only changes if the scope does.

How does working with you start?

With a free 15-minute pre-audit call where you describe the CRM, the team and what keeps going wrong. I then send a written list of needs for you to correct, followed by a fixed quote.

Which CRMs and tools do you work with?

HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive, with automations in Make, n8n or native CRM workflows, and dashboards in HubSpot, Salesforce or Power BI. I hold 8 HubSpot certifications and Make Certified Advanced, and I build AI agents on the Claude API.

What happens after the audit?

You keep the action plan. Your team can run it, I can rebuild and automate the CRM as a fixed-price project, or I can work on it one day a week. Nothing in the audit commits you to the next step.

What kind of companies do you work with?

B2B SaaS companies, established SMBs and larger sales teams using HubSpot, Salesforce or Pipedrive. Over about two years at Turnk I ran these CRMs for 8 B2B client teams.

When does a company need interim RevOps instead of a full-time hire?

When the CRM needs an owner but a full-time RevOps hire is hard to justify yet. I work one day a week with a weekly check-in and a monthly written report, for three months minimum, and leave a handover document for the person you hire next.

Where are you based, and do you work in French?

I am based in Toronto and work remotely with B2B companies across Canada and the US. Calls, documents and training can be in English or French, which is my first language.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes. I take HubSpot and automation work as a subcontractor for HubSpot agencies, under your brand if you prefer.

So, when do we start?

15 minutes to look at your CRM together and see which offer fits. No commitment.
